ROO Secure IT Solutions — Data & Compliance Framework

Operational privacy and compliance framework for ROO Secure applications and services.

1. Compliance Frameworks

This document describes the compliance framework used to organise privacy and data-protection obligations. It is not a certification or legal opinion.

2. GDPR / UK GDPR

Where applicable, the organisation should maintain records of processing, identify controller/processor roles, document lawful bases, provide transparent notices, manage data-subject requests, assess international transfers, and maintain appropriate security measures.

3. CCPA / CPRA

Where applicable, maintain an inventory of California personal information, purposes, sources, disclosures, retention, rights processes, opt-out mechanisms and contracts with service providers/contractors as required.

4. CalOPPA

For covered online services, maintain an accessible privacy policy describing relevant information practices and update it as required.

5. Governance

Maintain a data inventory, vendor register, records of processing, retention schedule, incident-response process, privacy-request process and change-management process.

6. Security Controls

Security controls should be risk-based and appropriate to the information processed, including access management, encryption, logging, vulnerability management, backups, monitoring and incident response.

7. Review

This document must be reviewed when a new application, vendor, tracking technology, payment flow, data category, jurisdiction or processing purpose is introduced.
