ROO Secure IT Solutions — Cookie & Tracking Policy

Description of cookies and similar technologies used by ROO Secure services.

1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies, SDKs, local storage, pixels and similar technologies may store or access information on a device. They can support authentication, security, preferences, analytics and advertising.

2. Categories

Strictly necessary technologies may be required for core functionality and security. Preference, analytics and advertising technologies should be enabled only according to the applicable consent and privacy requirements.

3. Analytics

Analytics technologies may collect technical and usage information such as device characteristics, pages or screens viewed, events and diagnostic information. Actual vendors must be listed in the application-specific notice.

4. Advertising and Remarketing

Remarketing technologies may allow ROO Secure or advertising partners to measure campaigns and show relevant advertisements. Where required, consent or an opt-out mechanism must be provided.

5. Your Choices

Users may be offered a cookie-consent interface, browser controls, device-level controls or vendor opt-out mechanisms depending on the technology and jurisdiction.

6. Updates

The technology inventory should be reviewed whenever analytics, advertising, authentication, payment or embedded third-party services change.
